A reconstruction, not a recording. Nobody has a recording of Vedic Sanskrit. A clear modern voice reads the Sanskrit; the Vedic accent (high, low, falling) is laid over it as a gentle pitch contour from real Pāṇinian rules or the traditional accent marks. Phrasebook lines are pre-recorded; your own words are spoken live. Translation is machine translation into Classical Sanskrit.
